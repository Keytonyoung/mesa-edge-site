Good to Know

Common Questions

Do you travel to Aspen and the Roaring Fork Valley?

Yes — that's where most of our events are. Aspen, Snowmass, Basalt, Carbondale and Glenwood Springs. We tow the range over from Grand Junction and set up at your venue. More on valley events →

Is Mesa Edge Axe the same as AX'D BABE?

Yes. Mesa Edge Axe took over the Carbondale axe throwing operation formerly known as AX'D BABE and rebranded it. Same sport, same valley — now a fully mobile range that comes to your venue instead of the other way round. Read the short version →

Do guests need any experience?

None. Most people who throw with us have never held an axe. A coach runs the lanes the whole event, and first-timers are usually sticking throws within a few minutes.

What does an event cost?

Events start at $300 per hour with a two-hour minimum. Every event is quoted individually based on headcount, location and which setup you pick.

How much space do you need?

The trailer range is self-contained and parks in a lot, field, or wide driveway. The freestanding targets fit on a lawn or patio. Tell us the venue and we'll tell you which setup fits.

Will it damage the venue?

No. Both setups are freestanding — nothing gets driven into the ground and nothing is attached to a building. The trailer range keeps every throw inside a cage.

Can we throw at night?

Yes. The lanes are lit, so evening receptions and after-dark parties work fine.

How far ahead should we book?

Summer weekends in the valley go early. If you have a date, reach out — even if the details aren't settled yet.
