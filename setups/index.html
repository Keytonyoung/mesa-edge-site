The Showpiece

The Trailer Range

A fully enclosed throwing range built onto a trailer. It rolls in, parks, and becomes a fixture of the event rather than a side attraction. People walk over just to see what it is.

  • Caged lanes, so spectators can crowd in close and stay safe
  • Handles a bigger crowd rotating through
  • Self-contained: works in lots, fields and venue grounds
  • Lit lanes, so evening events keep going after dark
  • No power or water needed

The Backyard Favorite

Mobile Targets

Freestanding target lanes we carry in and set on the ground. The right call when a trailer cannot turn around, or when the party is already happening at somebody's house.

  • Sets up on grass, lawns and level ground
  • Nothing staked into the ground, nothing attached to the house
  • Relaxed, backyard-party feel
  • Good for birthdays, reunions, bachelor and bachelorette parties
  • Same axes, same coaching

Either Way

What comes with both

Targets, axes, safety setup, lighting and a coach who runs the lanes the entire event. We arrive early, set up out of the way of your timeline, and pack out clean when it is over. You do not lift anything.

Every thrower signs a waiver before they pick up an axe. The coach controls the throwing area the whole time and will turn away anyone who should not be throwing, which matters more at weddings than anywhere else.

Pricing is the same for both: $300 per hour, two-hour minimum, quoted per event once we know headcount, venue and travel.
