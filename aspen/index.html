The Short Version

Why there's no axe range in Aspen — and what to do about it

Axe throwing is a warehouse business. It wants cheap square footage, a tall ceiling and a parking lot, which is exactly what the Roaring Fork Valley does not have going spare. The valley's one axe range, the Carbondale operation that ran as AX'D BABE, is the one we took over — and rather than pay valley rent for a building, we rebuilt the whole thing on wheels.

That turns out to be better for events anyway. Your guests are already at the ranch, the rental house, the tent, the office. Making forty people drive somewhere in the middle of a reception is how you lose the room. We park the range where the party is, coach everyone who wants to throw, and pack it out when you're done.

We tow up from Grand Junction, which means the whole valley is a normal working day for us — Glenwood Springs, Carbondale, Basalt, Woody Creek, Snowmass and Aspen. We have set the range up at valley ranch venues including T Lazy 7 Ranch in Aspen.

Valley Questions

Aspen-area FAQ

Is there an axe throwing place in Aspen?

No — there's no permanent axe range in Aspen or anywhere in the Roaring Fork Valley. Mesa Edge is the mobile version: we bring the range to your venue instead.

Do you set up at private homes in Aspen and Snowmass?

Regularly. The freestanding targets go on a lawn, patio or courtyard, and the trailer parks in a driveway or turnaround if there's room to back in. Nothing gets staked into the ground and nothing is attached to the house.

Can you do a wedding reception?

Yes, and valley weddings are a big part of our summer. We've run the range at ranch venues including T Lazy 7 Ranch in Aspen. We set up before guests arrive so it's ready the moment the reception opens up.

What about corporate retreats and team events?

Works well — it's the rare activity where the people who aren't competitive still have a good time watching. Tell us your headcount and how long you have the group for.

Is there a travel charge up the valley?

Travel is built into the quote. You get one number for the whole event before you decide.

How far ahead should we book?

Summer weekends in the valley go early. If you have a date, reach out now — the details can come later.

Can it run into the evening?

Yes. The lanes are lit, so receptions and after-dark parties are no problem.
